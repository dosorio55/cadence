Cadence bundles the 30 MP3 files from this pinned source without changing
their bytes. Playback pitch-shifts samples to cover intervening notes and
scales their volume with the lesson's MIDI velocities. This reduced set does
not provide the original collection's sixteen recorded velocity layers.
